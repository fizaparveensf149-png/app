Matchday & Fan Guidelines

Gate Entry & Timings

Stadium gates open 2.5 hours before first ball. Carry a valid photo ID and digital or printed match ticket.

Banned Items

Outside food, beverages, lighters, drones, sharp objects, and large bags are not permitted inside the stands.

Fair Play & Code

Respect players, officials, and fellow spectators. Follow steward instructions during powerplays and innings breaks.
